Timeline

This the timeline page

Metal didn’t just appear fully formed — it grew, fractured, and reinvented itself year after year, leaving behind a trail of riffs, scenes, and breakthroughs that shaped everything we hear today. This timeline is built to show that evolution in motion. As you scroll downward, you move through the decades the same way metal did: from the early sparks that defined the genre’s identity, through the explosions of new subgenres, to the constant experimentation that keeps metal alive. Each point marks a moment where someone pushed the sound further, broke a rule, or created something no one had heard before. It’s less a straight line and more a living map of how metal kept expanding, mutating, and refusing to stay in one box. This page is here to make that journey visible — a way to watch the genre grow as you move through its history.
