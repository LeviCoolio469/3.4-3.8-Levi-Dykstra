Interviews

Toni Iommi (Black Sabbath)

Tony Iommi is the legendary guitarist and primary riff‑writer of Black Sabbath, widely credited as one of the founding architects of heavy metal guitar. His signature dark, downtuned sound came partly from necessity: after losing the tips of two fingers in an industrial accident as a teenager, he created homemade prosthetic fingertips and adapted his playing style, which ultimately shaped the entire genre.

In this interview, Iommi talks candidly about that accident, how he nearly quit music, and how Django Reinhardt inspired him to keep going. He also discusses his early bands before Black Sabbath, the local Birmingham music scene, and the gradual evolution of his sound. The video mixes personal storytelling with reflections on how his limitations pushed him toward the heavy, powerful style that would define Black Sabbath and influence generations of metal musicians.

James Hetfield (Metallica)

James Hetfield is the co‑founder, rhythm guitarist, and lead vocalist of Metallica, known for his precise down‑picking, powerful stage presence, and the commanding vocal style that helped define thrash metal. As one of metal’s most influential frontmen, Hetfield shaped the genre with his mix of aggression, discipline, and emotional honesty, becoming a central figure in the evolution of modern heavy music.

In this interview, he reflects on the surreal experience of performing massive outdoor shows, especially the legendary 1991 Monsters of Rock festival in Moscow. Hetfield talks about the sheer scale of the crowd, the energy of half a million people moving as one, and the responsibility that comes with commanding an audience that size. He also discusses how he stays grounded onstage, how Metallica approaches performances at that scale, and what it feels like to connect with so many people through music. The video blends personal reflection with behind‑the‑scenes insight into what it means to front one of the biggest metal bands in history.
