Toni Iommi (Black Sabbath)

Tony Iommi is the legendary guitarist and primary riff‑writer of Black Sabbath, widely credited as one of the founding architects of heavy metal guitar. His signature dark, downtuned sound came partly from necessity: after losing the tips of two fingers in an industrial accident as a teenager, he created homemade prosthetic fingertips and adapted his playing style, which ultimately shaped the entire genre.

In this interview, Iommi talks candidly about that accident, how he nearly quit music, and how Django Reinhardt inspired him to keep going. He also discusses his early bands before Black Sabbath, the local Birmingham music scene, and the gradual evolution of his sound. The video mixes personal storytelling with reflections on how his limitations pushed him toward the heavy, powerful style that would define Black Sabbath and influence generations of metal musicians.

James Hetfield (Metallica)

James Hetfield is the co‑founder, rhythm guitarist, and lead vocalist of Metallica, known for his precise down‑picking, powerful stage presence, and the commanding vocal style that helped define thrash metal. As one of metal’s most influential frontmen, Hetfield shaped the genre with his mix of aggression, discipline, and emotional honesty, becoming a central figure in the evolution of modern heavy music.

In this interview, he reflects on the surreal experience of performing massive outdoor shows, especially the legendary 1991 Monsters of Rock festival in Moscow. Hetfield talks about the sheer scale of the crowd, the energy of half a million people moving as one, and the responsibility that comes with commanding an audience that size. He also discusses how he stays grounded onstage, how Metallica approaches performances at that scale, and what it feels like to connect with so many people through music. The video blends personal reflection with behind‑the‑scenes insight into what it means to front one of the biggest metal bands in history.

Rob Halford (Judas Priest)

Rob Halford is the iconic frontman of Judas Priest, known for his soaring vocal range, leather‑and‑studs aesthetic, and the commanding presence that helped define heavy metal’s identity. His sharp precision, theatrical delivery, and fearless individuality earned him the title “The Metal God,” making him one of the most influential singers in the history of the genre. Across decades, Halford has shaped metal not just through sound, but through attitude, style, and the unapologetic intensity he brings to every performance.

In this interview, Halford sits down with Sammy Hagar to talk openly about the wild, chaotic world of rock and roll, reflecting on the highs and lows that come with life in a legendary metal band. He discusses the culture of the era — the drugs, the pressure, the escape, and the toll it can take — while also sharing how he eventually found clarity and stability. Halford’s honesty gives the conversation real weight, balancing humor with hard‑earned wisdom. The video blends stories from the road with personal reflection, offering a candid look at how one of metal’s greatest voices survived the excess and came out stronger.

Ronnie James Dio (Dio)

Ronnie James Dio is one of metal’s most iconic vocalists, known for his powerful operatic voice, mythic lyricism, and the larger‑than‑life presence that helped define the genre’s epic, fantasy‑driven side. From Rainbow to Black Sabbath to his own band Dio, he shaped metal with a mix of technical mastery, emotional depth, and theatrical flair. He’s also credited with popularizing the “devil horns” hand gesture, cementing his status as a cultural pillar of heavy metal and one of its most beloved figures.

In this clip, Dio takes over as host of MTV’s Headbangers Ball, bringing his trademark charm, humor, and storytelling to the show. He talks about life on the road, the energy of metal fans, and the spirit that drives the genre forward, all while introducing videos and sharing behind‑the‑scenes insight from decades in the scene. The segment captures Dio at his most charismatic — warm, witty, and effortlessly commanding — offering a rare look at how he connected with fans not just through music, but through personality and presence. It’s a snapshot of metal history delivered by one of its greatest voices.
